# Goal Reasoning

When you create a goal in Blueprint, the Goal Reasoning system immediately generates a complete strategic plan. This happens synchronously when the goal is saved — by the time you click through to the goal detail page, the plan is ready.

## What the system produces

### Feasibility assessment

A 0–100 score with a plain-English explanation. Blueprint looks at:

- Your current connector data (traffic, revenue, rankings, conversions)
- The goal target and deadline
- Historical rate of change for the relevant metric
- Whether the implied growth rate is realistic given past performance

A score of 80+ means Blueprint considers the goal achievable on the timeline. Below 60, it will explain what's holding the score down — usually that the implied weekly growth rate significantly exceeds recent history.

### Strategic paths

3–5 distinct approaches to reaching the goal, each with:

- A short label (e.g. "Content velocity", "Paid acquisition push", "Conversion rate focus")
- A 2–3 sentence description of the approach
- A rough resource estimate
- A confidence rating

Green paths are recommended. Grey paths are alternatives with lower probability. Amber paths have explicit constraints (e.g. "requires budget increase").

### Milestone sequence

A series of intermediate checkpoints with measurable criteria. For a revenue goal, milestones might be:

- **Week 4**: MRR at £X (leading indicator: new trials up 20%)
- **Week 8**: MRR at £Y (conversion rate holding above 3%)
- **Week 12**: Goal achieved

Milestones are tracked automatically — if connector data shows you're on track, the milestone card turns green. If you're behind, it turns amber with a note on what's lagging.

### Agent briefings

Which agents are most relevant to the goal, and what they should focus on. For a traffic goal:

- SEO Sentinel → prioritise keyword opportunities with >500 impressions and CTR <2%
- Quill → brief two content pieces per week targeting the traffic gaps
- Trend Spotter → surface seasonal keywords to target

These briefings influence how agents prioritise their proposals during runs. The briefing is injected into each agent's system prompt context on subsequent runs and looks like this in the LLM context:

```
## Active goal context
Goal: Grow organic sessions to 15,000/month by September 2026
Your focus: Surface high-impression, low-CTR keywords — these are the fastest path to the traffic target.
Avoid: Proposing content for keywords already ranking in positions 1–5.
```

### Conflict check

Blueprint checks whether a new goal conflicts with existing active goals. Common conflicts:

- Two goals pulling on the same metric in opposite directions
- Goals with overlapping measurement windows that will pollute attribution
- Resource conflicts (e.g. two goals both requiring Quill to produce 4+ pieces per week)

Conflicts are flagged with a warning banner. You can acknowledge them or modify a goal to resolve them.

> [!WARNING]
> Running two conflicting active goals simultaneously pollutes Blueprint's attribution data. If both goals require changes to the same pages or metrics within overlapping timeframes, Blueprint cannot determine which goal's actions caused a given metric movement. Resolve conflicts before activating a second goal, or accept that attribution quality will be reduced for both.

## Scenario planning

For any active goal, click **Run Scenarios** on the goal detail page to model what-if futures.

Blueprint generates 3–4 scenarios side-by-side. For example, for a revenue goal:

| Scenario | Assumption | Month 3 Projection |
|----------|-----------|-------------------|
| Base case | Current growth rate continues | £18,400 MRR |
| Accelerated content | +2 posts/week, SEO compounding | £22,100 MRR |
| Paid push | £3k/month Google Ads | £24,800 MRR |
| Retention focus | Reduce churn by 1.5% | £20,600 MRR |

Scenarios are stored and can be revisited. They don't affect agent behaviour — they're decision-support tools for you.

## Proactive goal suggestions

Blueprint scans your connector data weekly and surfaces goal suggestions automatically, visible as **Suggested Goals** on the Goals page.

Each suggestion includes:

- The opportunity identified (e.g. "17 keywords ranking 11–20 with >800 impressions/month")
- A specific, measurable goal framing
- An estimated £/month revenue impact
- Which connector data the opportunity came from

> [!TIP]
> Suggestions are generated by the Conductor during its weekly briefing cycle. If you're not seeing them, check that the Conductor ran successfully and that at least one connector has recent data.

## Reading a goal plan

The goal plan is on the goal detail page under the **Plan** tab.

- **Collapse/expand** sections using the chevron on each header
- **Hover a milestone** to see the metric it's tracking and current value
- **Click a strategic path** to see the full agent briefing for that approach

> [!NOTE]
> Goal plans are regenerated if you edit the target or deadline. Previous plans are kept in the audit trail.
