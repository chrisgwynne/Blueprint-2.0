AI agents that know your business

Each agent has a name, a role, and soul files — editable markdown documents that define their personality, values, and how they work. Run any LLM: Claude, GPT-4, Gemini, or Ollama locally.

Nothing happens without your say-so

Agents propose. You approve. Via the dashboard or a Telegram message. Green-tier tasks can auto-approve. Every action has a paper trail and a rollback button.

A brain that compounds

A git-backed knowledge base that grows smarter with every agent run, every signal, every insight. Based on the Karpathy LLM wiki pattern. Works with Obsidian vaults.

Any agent can connect

The Blueprint Agent Protocol lets any external agent — on any machine, any stack — participate as a peer. Register, read signals, propose tasks, write to the KB.

Built for

Solo founders running multiple projects who can't watch everything manually

Digital agencies managing client sites across GA4, GSC, Shopify, and more

E-commerce operators who need revenue signals and content automation

Anyone who wants their business watched 24/7 by agents they control

Your data stays yours

No SaaS subscription. No data leaving your server. Deploy on your machine, Hetzner, Coolify, or a Raspberry Pi. Credentials encrypted at rest. Full audit trail. MIT licensed.
