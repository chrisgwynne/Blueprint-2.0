The loop

How Blueprint works

A continuous operating loop that runs on your business around the clock — with you in control at every step.

  1. 01

    Connect your systems

    Securely link Shopify, GA4, Search Console, Brevo, GitHub, servers and more in minutes.

  2. 02

    Detect signals

    Blueprint watches your data and flags anomalies, drops, risks and opportunities as they happen.

  3. 03

    Create tasks

    Each signal becomes a clear, scoped task with a diagnosis and a recommended action.

  4. 04

    Execute real work

    Agents act through your connected tools — editing pages, opening PRs, shipping emails, tuning servers.

  5. 05

    Verify results

    Blueprint tests the outcome, gathers evidence, and confirms the change actually worked.

  6. 06

    Store knowledge

    Every decision, fix and result is saved to a permanent, searchable company knowledge base.

Beyond summaries

Real action, not summaries

Plenty of tools will summarise your numbers back to you. Blueprint goes further — it investigates the cause, takes the action, tests whether it worked, and reports back with evidence. The work gets done, not just described.

  • Investigates — traces the signal to a root cause across your systems.
  • Acts — executes the fix through your real, connected tools.
  • Tests — re-measures to confirm the change moved the metric.
  • Reports — writes up the outcome with before-and-after evidence.

In practice

Example workflows

Every workflow follows the same trustworthy path: Signal → Diagnosis → Action → Verification → Report.

Shopify

Sales drop detected

  1. Signal Revenue down 22% week-over-week.
  2. Diagnosis A best-seller variant is out of stock and hidden.
  3. Action Re-enable variant, surface back-in-stock badge.
  4. Verify Add-to-cart rate restored within 24h.
  5. Report Recovered ~£3.4k/wk; logged to memory.
Performance

Website speed issue

  1. Signal LCP crossed 4s on key landing pages.
  2. Diagnosis Unoptimised images and render-blocking JS.
  3. Action PR to lazy-load assets and inline critical CSS.
  4. Verify LCP 4.2s → 1.1s; CLS within budget.
  5. Report Bounce rate −9%; change documented.
SEO

Search ranking drop

  1. Signal Top query fell from position 3 to 11.
  2. Diagnosis Thin content and a missing canonical tag.
  3. Action Expand page, fix canonical, request re-index.
  4. Verify Impressions +40% over two weeks.
  5. Report Back on page one; insight stored.
Email

Campaign underperformance

  1. Signal Newsletter open rate dropped below 18%.
  2. Diagnosis Weak subject lines and poor send timing.
  3. Action Draft A/B subjects, reschedule to peak window.
  4. Verify Open rate 18% → 31% on next send.
  5. Report Revenue per email +24%; saved.
DevOps

GitHub / deployment error

  1. Signal Production deploy failing on CI.
  2. Diagnosis Broken migration and missing env var.
  3. Action PR with fix; flag rollback for approval.
  4. Verify Pipeline green; health checks pass.
  5. Report Incident timeline written to memory.
Conversion

Product page optimisation

  1. Signal High traffic, low conversion on a hero SKU.
  2. Diagnosis Missing reviews, weak above-the-fold copy.
  3. Action Add social proof, rewrite benefits, test layout.
  4. Verify Conversion +13% vs. control.
  5. Report Winning variant promoted; logged.
